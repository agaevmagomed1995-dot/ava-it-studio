ABOUT

Technology built around your business.

AVA IT Studio is a Dubai-based development studio creating websites, e-commerce stores, web applications and business automation.

Founded by a hands-on designer and developer, AVA brings design and engineering together — from the first conversation to launch and ongoing support. You work directly with the person building your solution, keeping communication clear and decisions focused on your business needs.

Our experience spans service marketplaces, online retail, logistics and recruitment platforms. Projects include BidFix, FORMA, FulfillPoint and the recruitment platform The Professional.
